## 👋 A little about me

I'm a software engineer who likes the moment a vague idea turns into something people can actually use. Most of my time goes into full-stack apps, and more and more into the place where AI meets everyday software: tools that give feedback, explain their reasoning, and make hard things feel simpler.

I learn by building, so almost everything below started as a "what if I tried…" and ended up as a real project.
